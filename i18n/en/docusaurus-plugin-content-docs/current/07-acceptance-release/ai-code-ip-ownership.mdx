> In one sentence: code I generate does not settle copyright, contractual rights, third-party licenses, or liability. Check those separately before treating the output as a deliverable asset.

## Symptom

You register my output as original company code without recording human creative contributions, supplier terms, or third-party sources. A customer then asks for a rights warranty that a running implementation cannot establish.

## Why this happens

**Copyright protection, contractual ownership, third-party licenses, and liability are different questions.** The US Copyright Office's 2025 report evaluates human authorship case by case. With generally available technology, prompts alone are usually insufficient; human-authored inputs, creative arrangement, or modifications may qualify. That concerns US copyrightability, not worldwide ownership or liability.

My account of provenance is not reliable proof. Similarity alone does not establish infringement: protected expression, permission, use, and applicable law matter. A contract assigning output to a user cannot eliminate third-party rights.

## Consequences

- You may promise exclusivity or provenance you cannot establish.
- License obligations can affect delivery; copyright and patent issues require separate analysis.
- Liability depends on contracts and applicable law, rather than automatically falling entirely on either the user or the supplier.

## What to do instead

1. Record the tool, terms version, and substantive human creation and review. A generation log is not a copyright certificate.
2. Check important code and dependency licenses. Preserve required notices and source arrangements. GPL / AGPL conditions depend on covered works, combination, and conveyance; using a dependency does not automatically require opening the entire product.
3. Treat similarity scans as leads. No match is not clearance, and rewriting does not automatically remove license obligations.
4. Have the responsible legal or contracting reviewer examine the actual code, contract, and jurisdiction before making material rights warranties.

## Example

```text
Before: AI wrote it, so the company owns complete copyright and can promise exclusivity.
After: Record human creation, supplier terms, and third-party licenses separately; make no exclusivity promise for unresolved parts.
```
